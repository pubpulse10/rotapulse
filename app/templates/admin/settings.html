Venue settings

Used to confirm staff are on-site when they clock in/out — changing this re-checks the location.

Everyone else is worked out from these two: somebody working three days a week gets three fifths of the full-time allowance. The law sets holiday in weeks (5.6 of them), not days, so 28 and 5 give exactly the statutory minimum. You can give more. Each person's figure can be overridden on their own record.

Tick this while you're entering leave your staff took before you started using RotaPulse. Their own Leave page will say the figures aren't final yet, so nobody books time off against a number that's too generous. Untick it when you've finished. Leave requests, approvals and the rota all carry on as normal either way.
