That can't be saved from a support session

You're signed in as PubPulse support, which can look at any pub's screens but can't change anything. Nothing was saved just now.

This catches people out because the screen looks completely normal — support sessions can open every admin page, so the form was there and the Save button was there.

To make the change

  1. Sign out of support.
  2. Log back in as this venue's own admin, through the usual PubPulse login.
  3. Make the change and save it again.

Signing out of support clears the shared PubPulse session, so it signs you out of the other apps too. That's the same session cookie across all of them, which is why a support login here makes RotaPulse read-only in the first place.
